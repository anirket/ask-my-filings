For the banking sector, this provides a supportive environment for credit growth and 
the productive deployment of capital. At the same time, evolving liquidity conditions, 
funding costs, inflation, and currency movements call for a continued financial 
discipline. We see significant opportunities ahead, but our approach remains firmly 
anchored in the quality of growth, prudent underwriting, and strong risk 
management.

improving the way we serve customers and deploy our resources. A key priority is to 
enhance productivity across our extensive branch network by progressively shifting 
routine banking transactions to digital and self -service channels. This allows our 
branches and employees to devote greater attention to customer engagement, 
relationship building, and business generation. 
 
I had mentioned some of the initiative under Project SARAL. Two major initiatives

• Re-KYC Program and 
• Seva Sarathi.

I would like to share a few thoughts on that. Our Re -KYC program illustrates the 
progress we are making on simplification of journeys. Since its launch in June, more 
than 1.2 crore, Re-KYC updates have been completed digitally reducing branch 
intervention by more than 50%, translating into 50,000 man -hours saved every day. 
These are meaningful productivity gains that can be redirected towards higher value 
customer engagement and business activities. 
 
We are seeing similar benefits from our Seva Sarathi initiative. More than 9,500 
Seva Sarathis are now deployed across high footfall branches, helping customers 
access routine services and migrate progressively towards digital and self -service 
channels. This is reducing branch -based servicing of routine transactions while 
delivering meaningful savings in servicing costs. 
 
Importantly, these initiatives are being implemented at scale, and their impact is 
being measured through tangible operating outcomes. This gives us a replicable 
framework for driving productivity and efficiency across other areas of the bank. 
 
Cyber resilience is another area where we are investing for the future. The recently 
inaugurated SBI CyRIS, our Cyber Resilience, Integrity, and Security Center of 
Excellence, will strengthen our capabilities while facilitating greater collaboration and 
knowledge sharing across the BFSI ecosystem. Collectively, these initiatives are 
aimed at building a more productive, technology -led, and scalable operating model 
for SBI.
