over the subsequent quarters upto Q2 of the FY 2028-29.

annual instalments not exceeding three, commencing from Q1 of the FY 2024-2025.

the Shareholders' account for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025 respectively.

over the subsequent quarters upto Q4 of the FY 2026-27.

charged to Shareholders' account over the subsequent quarters upto Q4 of the FY 2026-27.
